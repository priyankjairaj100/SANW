STUDY SPECIFICATION AND RECOVERED EVIDENCE RECORD

Everything in this file is retained conversation context, not a fresh reading
of the original raw experiment files. The old files are currently missing.

CENTRAL STORY
The strongest completed-evidence story was a controlled audit of what semantic
relation labels add beyond extra positive targets. The comparison fixes the
candidate pool, adapter capacity and tuning budget. It separates positive
expansion, contradiction weighting and neutral exclusion, and evaluates valid
alternative captions and full-pool retrieval.

Extra positives improve the supervised supported-versus-contradicted task.
The combined relation policy does not beat positive expansion. Its transfer
gain over source-positive adaptation is concentrated in added objects and
attributes. The combined replacement/swap group declines. Similarity weights
closely track shuffled or constant controls. These observations describe the
tested selected training procedures, not all relation-learning methods.

Generic compositionality/retention tension and the insufficiency of ordinary
extra positives were already established by close prior work. The novel
positioning therefore depended on the specific controlled intervention audit.

IMPLEMENTATION AND SCOPE
Frozen OpenCLIP ViT-B/32, laion2b_s34b_b79k; normalized 512-dimensional features.
Two zero-initialized 512-by-512 linear residual maps; 524,288 trained parameters.
Both encoders and score scale fixed. No full-encoder fine-tuning.

Image-disjoint e-SNLI-VE split: 1,200 training, 100 calibration, 100 validation,
400 test images. Five original source captions are positives for every
contrastive policy. The source-positive baseline also includes hypothesis
candidates as negatives, including supported hypotheses. Positive expansion
promotes supported hypotheses already in the pool. Contradiction weighting
uses weight 2. Neutral exclusion uses weight 0. Unannotated cross-image pairs
remain negatives with weight 1.

Twelve configurations:
clip, sanw_fixed, sanw_median, constant, shuffled, multipositive, grounded,
grounded_no_hardening, grounded_no_abstention, pairwise_rank,
random_exclusion, smoothing.

Each configuration: three learning rates, seeds 17/29/43, ten epochs, epoch
zero eligible. This gives 108 candidate runs. One learning rate is selected
per configuration by mean validation performance over seeds; each seed keeps
its best epoch at that rate. Validation averages known-positive image-to-text
R@1 and supported-versus-contradicted hypothesis accuracy. Evaluation uses 36
selected checkpoints and the frozen reference. Six selected checkpoints are
epoch zero, giving 31 unique parameter states including frozen.

EXACT LOSS CONVENTION
Image-row loss: log(sum_j w_ij exp(s_ij)) - mean_{j in P_i}(s_ij).
Uniform targets over positives. Positive candidate weights are one. Weights
and masks are detached. The text direction transposes the relation matrix,
uses its own positive normalization, and skips anchors without a positive.
Pairwise ranking averages softplus(s_contradicted - s_supported) over all
supported-by-contradicted pairs within an eligible image, then over images.
Target smoothing: 0.9 uniform(P) + 0.1 uniform(candidates).
Constant negative weight: 0.25. Random exclusion matches exclusion count.

EVALUATION
e-SNLI-VE: 400 held-out images; supported-versus-contradicted ranking, averaged
by image, ties receive half credit.
SugarCrepe: all 7,511 official pairs from 1,560 images, strict positive > foil.
SugarCrepe++: 4,757 triplets from 1,542 images, both valid captions > foil.
COCO Karpathy: 5,000 images, 25,000 captions, both retrieval directions over
the full pool. First five captions in source order; the source has 25,010.

Six planned primary contrasts: grounded against multipositive,
grounded_no_hardening, and grounded_no_abstention, each on two primary tasks.
10,000 paired image bootstrap replicates, bootstrap seed 20261002,
Bonferroni family size six; 99.1667% intervals condition on selected runs.
Other benchmark and control differences are secondary/descriptive.

SELECTED NUMERICAL RESULTS
Grounded minus multipositive:
  e-SNLI-VE: -2.67 percentage points, adjusted interval [-3.23, -2.12].
  SugarCrepe: -0.98 points, adjusted interval [-1.42, -0.52].
  e-SNLI per-seed differences: -1.01, -0.19, -6.81 points. Last seed frozen.

Multipositive minus source positives:
  SugarCrepe +0.940842 percentage points.
  COCO image-to-text R@1 -7.526667 points.
  Net extra failed image queries per 5,000: 316, 336, 477; mean 376.333.
  Lost previous successes: 539, 550, 742; new successes: 223, 214, 265.
  Net extra failed text queries per 25,000: 692, 630, 1,364; mean 895.333.

SugarCrepe++ relative to frozen:
  Multipositive original-positive accuracy 79.46 -> 80.12 percent.
  Alternative-positive accuracy 75.01 -> 73.05 percent.
  All 30 nonzero selected adapters decline on alternative positives and
  COCO text-to-image R@1. Original positives improve in 27 of these 30.
  Multipositive does not uniquely worsen the alternative-positive metric
  versus source positives: 73.05 versus 72.99 percent.

SugarCrepe grouping was a post hoc descriptive aggregation of saved scores,
not a new test family or checkpoint-selection criterion. Addition gains by
seed: +4.0305, +3.6674, +2.6144 points. Combined replacement/swap changes:
-0.3784, -0.3574, -0.7778. Some individual swap categories improve; do not
claim every replacement and swap category declines. SugarCrepe++ first-
positive scores must not substitute for SugarCrepe category scores.

Fixed-threshold and shuffled weights select the same rates and epochs in
every seed; median-threshold and constant controls do so too. Close scores
are not a statistical equivalence claim.

SEPARATE CLASSIFIER DIAGNOSTIC
Three-way logistic relation classifiers: multimodal macro-F1 66.28,
text-only 61.15, image-only 33.08. Calibration accepts 635/6,040 candidate
contradictions, of which 612 match the reference: precision 96.38%, coverage
10.51%. No accepted-positive branch. These predictions did not train the
primary adapters. Reference labels partly inherit text-inference judgments.

EARLIER DEVELOPMENT

SmolVLM inference smoke test. These are not primary natural-image evidence.

pooled with the newly executed adapter study.
