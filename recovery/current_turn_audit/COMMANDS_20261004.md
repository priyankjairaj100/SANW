## Certificate interpretation

A certificate applies to the exact evaluated gallery.
The KL direction is teacher to student at temperature two.
The diagnostic uses no temperature-squared multiplier.
Its threshold accounts for every relevant caption.
The drift certificate uses native logits and ignores common shifts.
Cross-relevance ties fail diagnostic correctness.
Stable benchmark ranks retain their original tie rule.

The audit reports coverage separately for trained and unchanged states.
A trained state requires a positive epoch and a nonzero update.
Unchanged states can have high coverage without any adaptation benefit.
Counts measure state-query observations over repeated galleries.
Coverage cannot establish caption improvement or guarantee another gallery.
Floating-point checks are not interval-arithmetic proofs.
