SANW: SCIENTIFIC CONTEXT

Research question

Can compositional adaptation improve retrieval while preserving specified baseline-correct training decisions?
The paper needs a narrow method claim and direct evidence of useful held-out performance.
Better composition alone is insufficient if retrieval deteriorates beyond the fixed margin.

Method

Keep the pretrained image and text encoders fixed.
Normalize their original embeddings.
Fit PCA projections and centering statistics using training data only.
Add a bilinear correction to the original similarity:

s_A(v,t) = v^T t + x(v)^T A y(t).

The correction has rank budget 128.
The frozen recipe uses R = 1 and composition weight 0.25.
Its ridge coefficient is 0.01.
Its retention fraction is gamma = 0.5.

Retain at least half each protected baseline-correct training margin.
Compare against the full training gallery.
Use the frozen index-based rule for ties.
For image-to-text retrieval, choose a fixed best-owned positive anchor.
That anchor defines a conservative convex branch of the multi-positive correctness set.

Theory that is supported

The fixed-anchor constraints define a convex feasible set.
Ridge regularization gives strong convexity and a unique optimum under the stated objective.
The zero correction is a feasible reference under the stated construction.
An exactly feasible selected state preserves the protected training decisions.
The additive map can be applied to queries using exact algebra.

Theory that is not established

No guarantee protects arbitrary unseen queries.
No theorem ensures a nonzero useful optimum.
No theorem proves convergence of the finite optimizer used here.
The fixed-anchor branch need not contain every correction that preserves multi-positive retrieval.
Query-only implementation does not establish a new gallery-reuse principle.

A proposed tangent-cone diagnostic uses the projected composition gradient.
That idea follows standard convex analysis.
It is not implemented or empirically validated.
It must not appear as completed evidence.

Frozen training data

TRAIN6000 contains 6000 images and 30000 source captions.
The training representation contains 110516 text or pair rows and 104963 unique strings.
Twenty-two exact source strings occur under multiple owners and remain included.
Three exact contradiction conflicts were excluded during training construction.
The eligible composition set contains 5979 owners and 746320 triples.

TRAIN6000 changed data, update count, gallery, and PCA relative to V9.
Its comparison with V9 does not isolate the effect of training size.
Original feature archives contain mixed splits.
The numerical fit uses the training subset.
Do not claim that held-out bytes were never loaded.

Frozen optimization

Use seeds 17, 29, and 43 for both encoders.
Run 32 epochs with batch size 64 and 3008 updates.
Use learning rate 0.03 / sqrt(epoch).
Use the specified feasibility tolerance of 1e-12.
Select the minimum nonzero feasible training objective.
Resolve selection ties using the earliest epoch.
Do not select using development or benchmark outcomes.

Novelty positioning

Bilinear ranking itself is established, including OASIS.
Linear adaptation of frozen CLIP features is established, including LABCLIP.
Joint composition and retrieval improvement is studied by CLIC.
Capability preservation is related to FSC.
Prediction compatibility and non-regression connect to PCT and RCAT.
Backward-compatible gallery use is studied by BCT.

The defensible candidate contribution is the specific retention construction and its audited empirical behavior.
Novelty requires a direct comparison with these neighboring ideas.
A combination of known pieces is not automatically a novel contribution.

Original review issues that still matter

Supported positive labels lacked a count-matched promotion control.
The old selection objective differed from the reported large-gallery retrieval metric.
The old work lacked an in-domain full-gallery retrieval evaluation.
Training selection and deployment evaluation must remain clearly distinguished.
These concerns motivate the frozen full-gallery program and the additional controls.

Short-paper design

Aim for one construction, one guarantee, and one decisive empirical finding.
Place related work after the introduction.
Keep the main body within the verified four-page short-paper limit.
Use appendices for complete proofs, protocols, audits, and secondary outcomes.
Do not compress unsupported claims into the abstract.
Do not promise acceptance, novelty, or superiority before the evidence supports them.
