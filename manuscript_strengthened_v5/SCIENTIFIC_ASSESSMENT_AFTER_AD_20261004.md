# Scientific assessment after the AD study

The four-page diagnostic paper remains scientifically coherent.
The requested practical improvement remains unsolved.
These statements describe different outcomes.

## Supported narrative

The paper asks what caption identity adds when promotion counts, similarity groups, schedules, and candidate captions stay fixed.
The original linear study and both new replications address that question.
Supported supervision reduces image-to-caption retrieval against Source at every tested rate in all three settings.
Caption-to-image benefits against stratified promotion recur, with one unresolved nonlinear rate.
This evidence extends beyond one adapter and one pretrained encoder.

The AD study then separates allocation from distillation at a common development-selected schedule.
Both interventions have positive adjusted main effects on both retrieval directions for both encoders.
Distillation also improves SugarCrepe++ within those matched cells.
No positive interaction is established.
These findings concern matched interventions, not each selected procedure's practical success.

The exact rank-retention theory describes the divergence needed for failure on a specified gallery.
It does not convert minibatch KL into an unseen-gallery guarantee.
Empirical certificate coverage remains pending.

## Negative practical result

None of six selected AD-study families passes the declared practical criterion on either encoder.
The failed requirements have different meanings.

| Outcome | Supported interpretation |
|---|---|
| Supported selects epoch zero for every seed. | This procedure retains frozen initialization; it does not demonstrate trained improvement. |
| RN50 allocation includes one epoch-zero seed. | This procedure fails the all-seeds-trained requirement. |
| ViT AD loses 1.47 caption-to-image points, with interval [-2.91, -0.02]. | This endpoint has an adjusted retrieval decline against frozen initialization. |
| Other AD retrieval intervals contain zero and extend below -1 point. | They do not establish one-point retention or prove a large retrieval loss. |
| Every family fails the SugarCrepe++ gain requirement. | None establishes the declared caption improvement. |
| AD gains 6.67 and 9.02 relation-accuracy points. | These gains cannot substitute for SugarCrepe++ improvement. |

The finite grid does not prove that practical improvement is impossible.
The paper must not replace the frozen criterion after seeing these results.
The original-retention results and certificate diagnostics cannot substitute for a passed practical criterion.
