\section{Experimental Design}
\label{sec:newexperiments}
\paragraph{Three adaptation settings.}
The starting encoder is OpenCLIP ViT-B/32 pretrained on LAION-2B \citep{cherti2023reproducible}.
Its original linear adapters have 524,288 trainable parameters.
The nonlinear replication uses separate dimension-to-128-to-dimension ReLU branches with zero output initialization.
The second encoder is OpenAI RN50 with separate linear adapters \citep{radford2021clip}.
Its architecture, feature dimension, and pretraining differ from the starting encoder.
All encoders remain frozen.
The adapters change their normalized image and text representations.

Each replication crosses five assignments, three learning rates, and three training seeds: 45 fits per setting.
The assignments are Source, Supported, and three score-stratified draws.
The rates are $10^{-4}$, $3\times10^{-4}$, and $10^{-3}$.
The seeds are 17, 29, and 43.
Epoch ten defines every primary assignment comparison.
The original linear study contributes 72 completed fits with its additional count-only controls.

\paragraph{A separate preservation study.}
The frozen retention design adds 180 fits across the two encoders.
It includes directional interventions, allocation, distillation, and nine matched randomized distillation controls per encoder.
The AD study uses linear adapters for both encoders and the same 1,200 training images.
It crosses $\lambda\in\{.5,.8\}$ and $\beta\in\{1,4,16\}$ with the declared rates and seeds.
The complete design includes U, A, D, AD, Source, interpolation, and matched randomized AD controls.
It requires 126 fits per encoder, including nine randomized controls.
Each base candidate has ten epochs and an epoch-zero reference.
Matched controls stop at the corresponding AD-selected epoch.
Appendix~\ref{app:newmethods} specifies the full grid and selection rules.

\paragraph{Development selection.}
The development pool contains 900 images and 4,500 source captions.
It excludes the original 100 calibration images.
The original 100-image validation subset supplies the relation-ranking objective.
For each candidate and seed, selection maximizes relation accuracy subject to both retrieval constraints.
Each retrieval direction can lose at most one percentage point relative to the frozen model.
The procedure chooses shared coefficients and a shared learning rate across seeds.
Exact ties favor the earlier epoch, lower rate, and lower coefficient.
We also report the separately declared zero-loss sensitivity analysis.

\paragraph{Search and caption discrimination.}
An image query must retrieve one of its five source captions: I$\to$T.
A caption query must retrieve its owning image: T$\to$I.
Recall at rank one, R@1, counts queries whose highest-scoring candidate is relevant.
The test pool contains 1,000 disjoint e-ViL images and all 5,000 source captions.
Both directions search the complete pool.

SugarCrepe tests a valid caption against an edited incorrect caption \citep{hsieh2023sugarcrepe}.
SugarCrepe++ requires two valid captions to both outrank the same incorrect caption \citep{dumpala2024sugarcrepepp}.
We also report relation accuracy on 400 images and COCO retrieval across 5,000 images and 25,000 captions.
Each task keeps its original relevance definition.

\paragraph{A joint practical criterion.}
Every selected seed must contain a nonzero trained update.
Both adjusted retrieval bounds must exceed $-1$ percentage point relative to frozen initialization.
The adjusted SugarCrepe++ gain must exceed zero.
The same family must pass for both encoders to support a replicated practical improvement.

The preservation analysis adjusts jointly over 80 primary contrasts across both encoders.
The matched four-cell analysis has a separate 24-contrast family.
Both use 100,000 paired image-cluster bootstrap resamples.
The earlier retention design retains separate 80-contrast preservation and 36-contrast directional families.
Each replication uses its own predeclared 12-contrast family.
All five caption queries stay with their owning image during resampling.
Intervals condition on the fitted seeds, assignment draws, and fixed galleries.
